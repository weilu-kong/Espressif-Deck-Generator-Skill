# Japanese Technical Copywriting

## Voice
Write like an experienced FAE / solution architect: precise, calm, useful and engineering-oriented.

Prefer:
- `〜を実現`
- `〜に対応`
- `〜をサポート`
- `〜に最適`
- `〜を低減`
- `〜を簡素化`

Avoid repetitive marketing superlatives or unsupported absolutes such as `完全`, `圧倒的`, `革命的`.

## Natural terminology
Avoid literal Chinese-to-Japanese technical translations.
Examples:
- commissioning → `コミッショニング`
- host controller → `ホストコントローラ`
- single-core → `シングルコア`
- distribution/transmission → contextually choose `配信`, `送信`, `設定・配信`

## Latin / number spacing
Do not mechanically insert spaces everywhere.
Natural forms include:
- `2026年6月`
- `1チップ`
- `3種類`

Technical house style may use spacing where it improves scanability:
- `Wi-Fi 6 対応`
- `ESP32-C5 を採用`
- `AI モデル`

Use one consistent style per deck and prioritize natural Japanese.

## Slide copy
- Headline: short claim or topic, not a paragraph.
- Body: one idea per line/paragraph.
- Bullets: 2–4 per card, usually one line each.
- Prefer concrete benefit + reason over slogans.
